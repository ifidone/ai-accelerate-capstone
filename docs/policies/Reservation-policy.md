# Reservation Rules

## Requesting a Reservation

A checkout request can be made for immediate pickup or scheduled for a
future date, as long as the request is made at least 12 hours in advance for
scheduled pickups. Same-day requests for items that are currently available
are auto-approved for students in good standing (no overdue items, under
the 2-item cap). Requests for items that are currently checked out by
someone else can be queued, and the requester is notified automatically as
soon as the item is returned and becomes available.

## Priority During Deadline Weeks

During weeks with a published assignment deadline for a course that uses a
given piece of equipment (for example, oscilloscopes during cache lab
measurement week), reservation requests tied to that course get priority in
the queue over general/exploratory requests. This is decided by the lab
manager tagging the relevant items as "priority" for the deadline window,
not automatically by the system. Outside of a tagged priority window,
requests are handled strictly first-come, first-served.

## Cancellations

A pending or scheduled reservation can be cancelled by the student any time
before pickup with no penalty. Repeatedly requesting and then no-showing for
scheduled pickups (three or more times in a semester) may result in the lab
manager requiring same-day-only requests from that student going forward,
since a habitually unclaimed reservation blocks the item for everyone else
in the queue.

## Multi-Item Reservations

A single reservation request covers one item. A student who needs several
different pieces of equipment for the same session (for example, an ESP32
kit and a sensor kit together) should submit separate requests for each
item; there is no bundled "kit" reservation type at this time.
