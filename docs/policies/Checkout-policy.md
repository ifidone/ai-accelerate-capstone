# Checkout Policy

## Standard Checkout Duration

Most items may be checked out for up to their listed `checkout_limit_days`,
shown in the inventory record. As a rule of thumb: microcontrollers and dev
boards (ESP32 kits, Arduinos) may be held for up to 3 days. Oscilloscopes and
other shared bench instruments may be held for up to 2 days, since demand for
them is high during lab weeks. Peripherals and sensor kits with lower demand,
such as thermal printers and environmental sensor kits, may be held for up to
5 days.

Students may have at most 2 items checked out at the same time. This cap
exists because in past semesters a small number of students checking out
most of the oscilloscope stock left nothing available during crunch weeks.
The 2-item cap applies per student, not per course.

## Renewals

A student may request one renewal per item, extending the due date by the
same number of days as the original checkout window. Renewal requests must
be made before the item is overdue. An item that is already overdue is not
eligible for renewal — it must be returned first, at which point the student
may immediately request a new checkout if the item is still needed and no
one else has a pending request for it.

## Late Returns & Holds

An item not returned by its due date is marked overdue. A student with any
overdue item is placed on hold and cannot check out additional equipment
until all overdue items are returned. Being on hold does not cancel existing
active checkouts — it only blocks new ones.

If an item is more than 5 days overdue, the lab manager will reach out to
the student directly and may escalate to the course instructor if there is
no response within 2 business days.

## Lab Manager Overrides

A lab manager may override the standard duration or the 2-item cap for a
specific student on a case-by-case basis — for example, to support a
capstone project that genuinely requires longer access to a piece of
equipment. Overrides should be recorded in the checkout notes so there is a
record of why the exception was made. Students cannot self-approve
overrides; only a lab manager can grant one.
